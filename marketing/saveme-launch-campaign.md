# SaveMe.Space Marketing Campaign

Campaign goal: move SaveMe from quiet build mode into public proof, trust, and signup conversion.

Live product promise verified from saveme.space:
- Capture voice. Get structured memory.
- Brain Dump is the best starting point.
- Nova organizes thoughts into notes, action items, and searchable memory.
- Agent memory angle is live on the landing page.
- Signup CTAs are live.

Primary audience:
1. Founders and builders with scattered thoughts, tasks, and ideas.
2. AI-agent users who keep repeating context to tools.
3. Productivity-minded professionals who hate manual organization.
4. Early adopters who want voice-first capture and searchable memory.

Positioning:
SaveMe.Space is not another notes app. It is a voice-first memory layer for humans and their AI agents.

Core message:
Your brain was not built to remember everything. Speak naturally, let Nova structure it, then let you and your trusted agents retrieve it later.

Campaign pillars:

1. Memory pain
People are drowning in notes, screenshots, emails, messages, and half-finished thoughts. The pain is not capture alone, it is retrieval and structure.

2. Voice-first capture
Typing creates friction. Voice captures thought before it disappears.

3. Agent memory
AI agents are weak when they start from zero. SaveMe creates durable context humans and agents can share intentionally.

4. Build in public proof
Show the system getting cleaner: Nova consolidation, BrainDump, agent keys, signup, product cleanup, deployment.

5. Direct conversion
Only 10 to 15 percent of posts should directly ask people to try SaveMe. Most should teach the problem first.

30-day cadence:

X/Twitter:
- 2 posts per day minimum
- 1 insight post in the morning
- 1 product/build/proof post in the evening
- 3 to 5 high-quality replies per day to founders, AI builders, productivity, and agent workflow conversations

LinkedIn:
- 3 posts per week
- More professional framing, less punchy than X
- Focus on productivity, knowledge work, team memory, and AI workflows

Facebook personal:
- 2 to 3 posts per week
- More personal/off-brand, story-based, not polished startup copy

Launch sequence:

Week 1, Awareness and pain:
- Open loops drain attention.
- Notes fail because capture and retrieval are separated.
- Voice is the lowest-friction input.
- Agents need memory, not just tools.

Week 2, Product proof:
- BrainDump turns messy speech into structure.
- Nova organizes thoughts.
- Agent memory layer is live.
- Signup is open.

Week 3, Use cases:
- Founder brain dump.
- Personal memory vault.
- Meeting follow-up capture.
- AI agent context store.
- Idea capture before sleep, driving, walking, shower recovery.

Week 4, Conversion:
- Start free.
- No card needed.
- Use the demo.
- Try one voice dump today.
- Invite early users for feedback.

Metrics to track weekly:
- X impressions
- Profile visits
- Link clicks to saveme.space
- Signup starts
- Completed signups
- BrainDump starts
- Voice dump completions
- Free to paid conversion once billing is verified

Rules:
- No em dashes in X posts.
- Insight first, product second.
- Never overclaim privacy, encryption, agent integration, or billing beyond what is live.
- Preview every X post before posting.
- Use “US and Nigeria” or no location. Do not mention Charlotte publicly.

Immediate first campaign angle:
Persistent memory for humans and agents.

Why this first:
The latest public X post already set up the idea that agents need better memory. Continue that thread instead of abruptly hard-pitching.
