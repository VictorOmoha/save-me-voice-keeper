# SaveMe Voice Keeper — Terms of Service

**Status:** Pending legal approval — not effective

**Draft prepared:** 2026-08-11

**Version:** SAVE-103 engineering draft 1

> **Human legal gate:** This engineering draft aligns product claims with implemented behavior and approved engineering decisions. It is not legal advice and must not be published or treated as effective until qualified counsel and the product owner approve it.

The canonical product-facing draft is implemented in `src/pages/TermsOfService.tsx`. It covers service limitations; account and acceptable-use basics; Google Gemini voice/AI processing; automatic memory extraction; connected-agent and webhook implications; the approved Free/Basic/Premium monthly catalog; Stripe card-only billing; no paid trial; no partial-month refunds except where required by law; cancellation without deletion; current limited JSON export; and current support-directed deletion versus the unimplemented D-003 target.

Unsupported claims for MFA, paid trials, PayPal, annual billing, Pro/Teams/Enterprise launch plans, complete backup, immediate/exact deletion, complete multi-format export, automatic self-service revocation, and unconditional refunds have been removed from the product-facing engineering draft.

## Required legal decisions before effective terms

Counsel must approve assent mechanics; operator/contact details; age and jurisdiction rules; intellectual-property provisions; suspension/termination rights; warranty, liability, indemnity and dispute language; consumer cancellation/refund requirements; notice/change procedures; privacy-policy incorporation; and effective/version metadata.
