3. LinkedIn pain post

Knowledge work has a memory problem.

Important information gets scattered across notes, emails, messages, meetings, screenshots, and half-finished documents. The issue is not that people do not capture anything. The issue is that captured information becomes hard to retrieve when it matters.

That is why I believe the next serious productivity layer will combine three things:

1. Low-friction capture
2. Automatic structure
3. Context retrieval for humans and AI agents

SaveMe.Space is my attempt at that layer.

The user should not need to perfectly organize a thought before saving it. They should be able to speak naturally, let the system structure it, then retrieve it later through search or agent workflows.

That is where the product is going.
